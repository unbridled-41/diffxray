# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in DiffXray a harassment-free, constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:
- Demonstrating empathy, technical honesty, and respect toward other contributors and users
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code review feedback
- Focusing on reproducible evidence, clarity, and engineering craftsmanship

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, or spam/astroturfing in community channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
